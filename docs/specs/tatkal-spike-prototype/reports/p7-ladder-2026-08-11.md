**R8 answer:** the status endpoint becomes a co-equal load stream, not a
collapse: its p99 wait tracks (and at drain-heavy exceeds) the booking
stream's because both queue on the same workers, tripping the
pre-registered wait-comparison clause in 20/20 drain-heavy runs — while
capacity share stays below the 50% clause (max ~39% at 0.25 s polling).
Sub-second polling is what turns the stream into a storm; at >= 1 s the
queue drains faster than clients poll. Antigravity's hypothesis is
PARTIALLY CONFIRMED: the waiting room does not collapse the server, but
its status stream is the marginal capacity consumer, scaling inversely
with polling interval.
